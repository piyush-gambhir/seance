# SÉANCE — The Viral Demo Video

The submission is a **viral-style video of the working product**. This is the
script, shot list, and the production tricks that make the "wait, how did it know
that?" moment land. Target: **one uncut hero take**, cut to **60–85s**.

---

## The core principle

The magic is **specificity + reaction in one breath**: she names a *real, visible
detail* of your object, and then catches your *real* reaction to it. Everything
below protects those two beats. End on the human's face, not the logo.

---

## Script (60–85s)

**[0–3s — HOOK. Cold open, no logo.]**
Tight on a real person's face mid-laugh. On-screen text: **"it named the chip on my mug."**
We hear Esmé before we see her: *"…someone slammed this down. In ANGER."*

**[3–10s — REVEAL.]**
Cut to the candle-lit séance UI; Esmé in her gilt frame.
VO/text: *"It's not a filter. She actually sees what you hold up — and reacts."*

**[10–35s — MAGIC MOMENT. Single uncut take.]**
Person holds up a chipped coffee mug. Esmé's signature beat: slow blink, head-tilt,
sharp gasp. *"This vessel… has known SEVEN deadlines… and YES — the chip, on its
left side. That was rage, child."* Person bursts out laughing. Esmé, instantly:
*"You LAUGH — but the dead do not jest."*

**[35–55s — PROOF IT'S REAL.]**
Person smirks, tests her with a single shoe. Gasp. *"AH. You doubt me. This shoe…
walked AWAY from someone who loved you."* Genuine, unscripted jaw-drop. Hold on it.

**[55–70s — PAYOFF.]**
Quick montage: a deliberately random/ugly object → the theatrical hedge
(*"the spirits… recoil"*) for an honest laugh. The candle meter fills; Esmé
delivers the **final prophecy** tying the objects together. The **prophecy card**
slides in (second shareable artifact).

**[70–85s — CTA.]**
Close on the person's real laugh. THEN the logo: **"Summon a spirit at [link].
Built with D-ID + ElevenLabs."** End on the reaction.

## Shot list

1. **0–3s** — Extreme close-up, real face mid-laugh, natural light, no logo. Text overlay "it named the chip on my mug." Esmé's voice over black-to-face.
2. **3–10s** — The séance UI revealed: candlelight, vignette, gilt frame, Esmé materializing. One clean beauty beat.
3. **10–35s** — **Single uncut** wide-ish shot showing BOTH the person's hands (the mug) AND the screen. Signature tic + the "named the actual chip" line + the genuine laugh, no cuts.
4. **35–55s** — Same uncut framing; the single-shoe test + "walked away from someone who loved you"; hold on the unscripted jaw-drop. (Cuts here read as fake — keep it unbroken.)
5. **55–65s** — Proof-of-messy-input: a random/ugly object triggers the hedge. Shows graceful, honest failure — it reads as *credible*.
6. **65–78s** — Meter fills; final prophecy; the captioned prophecy card renders.
7. **78–85s** — Close on the genuine laugh, then logo + "Live at [link] · Built with D-ID + ElevenLabs."

## Production tricks (these win or lose it)

- **The "object gauntlet."** Rehearse a curated set: a chipped mug (rich, specific
  details), a single shoe (absurd backstory), a phone (universal), and one
  deliberately ugly random thing (for the hedge gag). Objects with visible,
  nameable detail give the vision model something undeniable to cite.
- **One uncut hero take.** The believability is that it's *not edited*. Show hands
  + screen together. Record several runs, keep the best single take for shots 3–4.
- **Frame the latency as ritual.** When you present an object, the app fires the
  "channeling" smoke + whisper instantly — let that beat breathe; it covers the
  think time and reads as character.
- **Lighting.** Light the *person* well (their reaction is the product). Keep the
  room dark enough that the candlelit UI glows.
- **Audio.** Headphones or a good mic with echo cancellation; her voice must be
  crisp. Capture the person's real laughter — emotional contagion is the share trigger.
- **Two artifacts.** The clip *and* the downloadable prophecy card. Show the card
  render; it's a second thing people screenshot.
- **Network.** Record the hero take on a known-good connection. Have a backup
  recording — never let a venue network sink the live take.
- **Captions.** Burn in captions (most social viewing is muted); the app's on-screen
  caption helps, but add punchy text for the hook and the killer lines.
